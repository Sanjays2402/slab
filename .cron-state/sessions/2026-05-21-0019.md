1. **v2.0.8's open-questions block explicitly named FreeText as a defer
   candidate**: "Text annotation (free-floating text box, not the v2.0.6
   sticky note)? Plan defers — closer to v2.0.10's 'Lathe' edit-mode
   pipeline." Promoting it to v2.0.9 (between Compose and Lathe) is the
   right placement — it's still an annotation primitive, not an edit-mode
   feature, so it belongs in the annotation family run.
2. **Acrobat-tier markup toolkit completion.** Acrobat, PDF Expert, Foxit,
   Drawboard, and Bluebeam ALL ship Typewriter as a paid feature. After
   v2.0.6 (highlight + note), v2.0.7 (ink), v2.0.8 (rect/ellipse/arrow/line),
   FreeText is the LAST missing annotation primitive vs the paid competitors.
   Closes the gap entirely.
3. **Architectural rhyme is exact.** v2.0.9 = pure additive variant
   extension of v2.0.6/v2.0.7/v2.0.8 plumbing: one new `Annotation::FreeText`
   variant in Rust, one new `kind: "freetext"` in TS, one new sub-mode in
   `DrawLayer.svelte`, one new `ActionId::AnnotText`. Zero new persistence
   layers, zero new Tauri commands, zero new deps. ~95% cost reuse.
4. **WOW story is strong**: single-key `t` + smart font autofit on overflow
   (shrink to 6pt floor, then box grows instead) + accent-tinted blinking
   caret + 220ms type-in fade-in. None of the paid competitors give you
   keyboard-driven Typewriter with autofit.

## Strategic positioning

After v2.0.9 ships, the **annotation primitive family is feature-complete**
vs every paid competitor on macOS, Windows, Linux. The next slice of the
roadmap (v2.0.10+, "Lathe" edit-mode pipeline) can focus on **interaction
polish** — selection handles, drag-to-move, drag-to-resize, multi-select,
clipboard, undo/redo across all annotation kinds — rather than adding more
primitive variants. This is the cleanest possible handoff between two
phases of the product.
