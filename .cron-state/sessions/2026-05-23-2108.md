## Debugging diary (for future Cake)

Three subtle bugs cost most of the time:

1. **lopdf skips `/Linearized` objects.** Its `Document::save_internal`
   filters out indirect objects whose `get_type()` returns `b"Linearized"`,
   `b"ObjStm"`, or `b"XRef"` — and `get_type()` falls through to the
   `/Linearized` key even when `/Type` is absent. Workaround: hand-roll
   the lin-dict serialization (`write_lin_dict_manually`). lopdf's
   `Writer` struct is private, so there's no clean way to call
   `write_object` directly on the lin dict.

2. **Sentinel-splice trick failed for the trailer.** I tried serializing
   the trailer dict via a throwaway one-object Document and splicing the
   `999999 0 obj ... endobj` slice. For high-numbered object ids lopdf
   silently switches to a cross-reference STREAM (no visible `obj`
   header) — the splice search panicked. Fix: hand-roll the trailer
   with a minimal `serialize_value` covering Name/Integer/Reference/
   Array/String/Dict.

3. **Inspector `/L <num>` collided with `/Linearized`.** Substring search
   matched the longer key first ("/Linearized") and tried to parse
   "inearized" as a number. Fix: `find_key` helper that requires a
   non-name-continuation byte after the key, plus bytewise tail parsing
   (UTF-8 trim was fragile against binary stream bytes).
