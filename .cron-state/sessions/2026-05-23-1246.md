- **Enterprise wedge:** Digital signatures are #1 ask after Forms for legal/IT.
- **Acrobat charges $239/yr** for digital signing; DocuSign starts $15/mo/user.
- **Natural follow-on to v3.9.0 Quill (Forms):** sign the forms you just filled.
- **Pure-Rust crypto** keeps cross-platform parity; no OpenSSL on Windows.
- **Self-signed today, CA-issued tomorrow:** ships valuable v3.10.0 without
  waiting on the trust-store + revocation UX (deferred to v3.10.1 Signet-LTV).
