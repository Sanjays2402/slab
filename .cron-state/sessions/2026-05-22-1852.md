## Why the artifact rename was needed

The Atlas (v2.2.0) + Theater (v2.3.0) tags both shipped while the
manifests still said `"2.1.2"`. CI artifact filenames embed the
manifest version, so the downloaded artifacts were named
`Slab_2.1.2_*`. For the v2.3.0 release I renamed them on copy to
`Slab_2.3.0_*` (cp, not rename — preserves the originals on disk).
The version bump in this commit prevents the issue recurring.
