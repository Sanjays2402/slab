# v2.4.0 — Stack

**See exactly what changed between two PDF revisions. Offline. Free.**

Adobe Acrobat Pro charges $239/yr for "Compare Files." Foxit Premium $129/yr. PDF Expert doesn't ship visual diff at all. Slab v2.4.0 puts it in the sidebar — under "Compare" — and ships it free, offline, and cross-platform.

## What's new

- **Side-by-side visual diff** — drop in two revisions, Slab renders both at the same DPI and lays a coral/mint change-box overlay on top of the rasters. Every changed pixel is flagged.
- **Scroll-locked panes** — when you scroll the old side, the new side follows. Toggle in the toolbar.
- **`n` / `N` to jump between changes** — 240 ms smooth-scroll snap to the next/previous change cluster. Reviewing a 60-page contract takes a minute, not an hour.
- **DPI / threshold / min-mass knobs** — tune the diff for noisy scans or pixel-perfect digital documents.
- **Command palette** — `Cmd/Ctrl+K` → "Compare" → done.
- **Detachable** — pop the Compare panel into its own window and keep the reader open alongside.

## Under the hood

- New `pdf::visual_diff` Rust module: Poppler `pdftoppm` raster → Rec.709 luma-delta mask → 3×3 dilate → BFS-flood-fill connected-component AABB extraction.
- New Tauri command `slab_visual_diff_pdfs(old, new, dpi?, threshold?, min_mass?)` returns a `VisualDiff` DTO that bundles base64 PNG rasters, change boxes, and the existing line-level diff in one round-trip.
- 7 unit tests pin the mask + AABB logic; one end-to-end test exercises the full Poppler pipeline (skipped gracefully when poppler isn't on the test runner).

## Privacy & cost, as always

- **Zero cloud.** Your PDFs never leave your machine. The diff happens in a Rust process on your laptop.
- **Zero telemetry.** Slab doesn't phone home.
- **Zero subscription.** Free, open-source, MIT-licensed.
