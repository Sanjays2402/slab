# Slab 3.32.0 — Atlas

**Your library, your way — Collections and Smart Collections arrive.**

Atlas turns Slab's library from a flat list into a real workspace. Group documents however your work actually organizes them — by case, by client, by project, by status — and let Smart Collections keep themselves up to date as you scan.

## What's new

- **Collections** — Create named groups like "Tax 2026", "Q2 contracts", or "Onboarding packets". A document can live in any number of collections without copying the file. Drop, drag, done.
- **Smart Collections** — Saved searches that re-evaluate every time you open them. "Recently added" auto-fills as you scan new folders. Build your own from any filter combo.
- **Animated count badges** — when a new document lands in a collection, its badge pulses so you notice. Acrobat just sits there. Linear-quality polish, built-in.
- **Defaults seeded** — "Recently added" and "All documents" smart collections come pre-loaded on first launch.
- **End-to-end** — backend SQLite tables, 11 Tauri commands, full client bindings, sidebar UI, persistent across restarts.

## Why it matters

Acrobat charges $239/year for cloud-only collections that ship your filenames to Adobe's servers. PDF Expert's "Folders" are Mac-only and don't have saved searches. Slab gives you both, offline, free, on all three platforms.

## Privacy reminder

Collections live in `~/.slab/library.sqlite` — never leave your machine.

## Install

- **macOS (Apple Silicon)** — `Slab_3.32.0_aarch64.dmg`
- **macOS (Intel)** — `Slab_3.32.0_x64.dmg`
- **Linux** — `Slab_3.32.0_amd64.deb` or `Slab_3.32.0_amd64.AppImage`
- **Windows** — `Slab_3.32.0_x64-setup.exe` (NSIS) or `Slab_3.32.0_x64_en-US.msi`

Free, open-source, offline-first.
