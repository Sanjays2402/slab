## What Loupe does

Drop any PDF — get back a per-rule pass/fail report against
**ISO 19005-2** (PDF/A-2b for archival, PDF/A-3b for e-invoicing),
plus actionable suggestions to fix what's wrong.

- **Per-level verdicts**: ✅ Compliant, ▲ Achievable with Bedrock
  conversion, ❌ Fail.
- **Font audit**: which fonts are embedded, which carry ToUnicode
  maps, which fall back to dangerous Standard-14 substitution.
- **Forbidden-entry preview**: shows you exactly which §6.6 entries
  (OpenAction, JavaScript, AA, …) Bedrock will strip during conversion.
- **ISO clause references** on every finding (e.g. `§6.2.2`,
  `§6.7.1`), so compliance officers can quote them in tickets.
- **Copy as Markdown**: turn the whole report into a paste-ready
  audit artifact for Jira, email, or a compliance ledger.
- **100% local**: the PDF never leaves your machine. Adobe's flow
  cannot make that promise.

## How to use it

1. Open Slab and pick **Loupe (PDF/A check)** in the sidebar, or
   press **⌘⇧I** (`Ctrl+Shift+I` on Windows/Linux).
2. Drag a PDF in or click to pick one.
3. Read the verdicts, fonts, suggestions.
4. Hit **Copy report as Markdown** to share with your team — or
   jump straight to Bedrock to convert.

## Under the hood

Loupe reuses every piece of the v3.0.0 Bedrock pipeline as a
non-destructive diagnostic:

- `font_audit` — read-only font embedding inspection.
- `sanitize_dry_run` — new in v3.0.1; clones the doc and reports
  what *would* be stripped without mutating the input.
- `validate_doc` — structural ISO 19005-2 conformance check, run
  once per level.
- `inspect_pdfa` orchestrator — unified report with auto-fixable
  classification and verdict logic.
