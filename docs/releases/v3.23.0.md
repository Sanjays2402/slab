# Slab v3.23.0 — Stack

**Compare two PDFs the way legal teams actually need to. Then share the
redline in a single file anyone can open.**

Litera Compare costs $400 per seat per year. Adobe Acrobat Pro charges
$239. Both ship the same core workflow: open two revisions of a
contract, mark every change, hand the marked-up PDF to a partner or
client for review. v3.23.0 is that workflow, free, fully offline, on
macOS, Windows, and Linux.

## Word-level redline, not "the whole line is yellow, hunt the change"

The Diff panel now diffs **at the word level inside every changed
line**. Where v3.22.0 told you "this line moved," v3.23.0 shows you the
six words that actually got added (green underline) and the four that
got removed (red strikethrough) — verbatim, in place, on the same row.

Drop two PDFs into the **Compare** panel (`⇧⌘D` from anywhere) and you
read the document the way a paralegal would: a clean line, the
inserts, the deletes, the line numbers from both sides in the gutter.
No more squinting at yellow bars.

## Export Redline — the part that closes deals

The new **Export Redline (.pdf)** button bakes the entire redline into
a single self-contained PDF you can email, drop in DocuSign, or attach
to a court filing. The recipient doesn't need Slab. They don't need
Litera. They don't need Acrobat Pro. They open the PDF in Preview,
Edge, Foxit, or anything else with a PDF viewer, and the green inserts
and red strikethroughs render natively because the markup is baked
into the file as colored text and stroke geometry — not as fragile
annotations that depend on the reader honoring them.

Every page that changed gets its own section with the page heading
(`Page 4` or `Old p.4 → New p.5` when pagination shifts), per-line
gutter numbers from both sides, and the word-level redline body.
Pagination wraps cleanly across the document. Cover page summarises
the deal — old path, new path, total +N -M ~K counts, and a legend.

This is what Litera charges $400/year for. Slab gives it away.

## Visual side-by-side, scroll-locked

For the cases where the diff lives in a chart, a signature block, or a
table that text-diff can't see, the **Stack** panel (the existing
visual diff) renders both PDFs at matched DPI, masks every changed
pixel, and lets you jump between change regions with `n` / `N`.
Scroll-lock keeps both sides aligned. `n` goes to the next change,
`N` back. Adobe Acrobat Pro Compare Files is the only mainstream tool
that does this — they charge $239/yr.

## Cmd / Ctrl + Shift + D, from anywhere

The Compare panel is one shortcut away from any other view. The
command palette (`⌘K`) has four new entries for Stack:

- **Stack: Compare two PDFs** — opens the panel
- **Stack: Export diff report (PDF)** — the markdown-style report
- **Stack: Export shareable redline (PDF)** — the buyer-magnet
- **Stack: Re-run last comparison** — diff the two files you just used

All four show up in the shortcut overlay (`⌘/`) under a new **Stack**
section.

## Why this matters

Most "PDF compare" tools price themselves at $200-$400/seat/year and
phone home to a corporate cloud. A 50-person legal team pays
$10,000-$20,000/year and ships every NDA, every settlement, every
M&A draft through a vendor's servers.

Slab Stack runs entirely on your machine. Zero network calls. Zero
files leaving. Zero seats to license. Same workflow.

## Everything else

- The `slab_stack_export_redline` Tauri command is now part of the
  stable command surface for plugin authors who want to wire redline
  export into their own workflows.
- All four Stack palette entries land in the command-palette MRU so
  the most-used one floats to the top automatically.
- Cover page renders even when the diff is empty — useful for the
  "I checked and nothing changed" handoff.
