Privacy policy

We collect nothing.

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and keeps no logs. There is no account system, no newsletter form, and nothing to opt out of — because there is nothing to opt into.

The Slab app itself has zero telemetry. It never phones home: no usage stats, no crash reports, no update pings. Your documents never leave your machine.

Downloads are hosted by GitHub, so fetching an installer is subject to GitHub’s privacy statement. The page itself is served by GitHub Pages. Neither sends us anything about you.

Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
